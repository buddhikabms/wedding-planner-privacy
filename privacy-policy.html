Privacy Policy — Wedding Planner

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy is written in English.

We collect nothing

We collect nothing. This app stores everything you enter — names, date, venue, budget, guests, tasks and lists — only on your own phone. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, and no data is sent anywhere.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

There are no ads and no analytics. The app does not track you, does not know who you are, and cannot see your data.

How the app works

The Ideas and Outfits galleries are bundled with the app; browsing them makes no network request. The Perfect Timing page calculates sunrise and sunset on your phone using maths — it does not ask any website for the answer.

Your data, your control

You can export a copy of your data from Settings, or delete it all from Settings. Deleting removes it from your phone permanently. There is no copy anywhere else to delete, because we never had one.

Contact

If you contact support, only what you choose to send is shared. You can reach us at bmscharmed@gmail.com.
