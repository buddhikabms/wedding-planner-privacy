Privacy Policy — Wedding Planner

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy is written in English.

Your wedding data stays on your phone

Everything you enter in the app — names, dates, venue, budget, guests, tasks and lists — is stored only on your own phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours, and your wedding data is never sent to Google AdMob or to any advertiser.

Advertising

The free version of Wedding Planner shows ads, served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and your approximate location, and — where you have given consent — may use that information to show personalised ads. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

On first launch in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows a consent form (provided by Google's User Messaging Platform, UMP) where you can choose personalised or non-personalised ads. If you refuse personalised ads, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change your choice later from the app's settings.

Premium is a one-time purchase of $14.99. Buying Premium removes all ads from the app.

No analytics, no tracking by us

We do not run analytics, we do not track you, we do not know who you are, and we cannot see your data. The only third party that receives any information from the app is Google AdMob, as described above, for the purpose of showing ads in the free version.

How the app works

The Ideas and Outfits galleries are bundled with the app; browsing them makes no network request. The Perfect Timing page calculates sunrise and sunset on your phone using maths — it does not ask any website for the answer. The only network content in the app is the advertising in the free version, loaded by Google AdMob as described above.

Your data, your control

You can export a copy of your data from Settings, or delete it all from Settings. Deleting removes it from your phone permanently. There is no copy anywhere else to delete, because we never had one.

Contact

If you contact support, only what you choose to send is shared. You can reach us at bmscharmed@gmail.com.
